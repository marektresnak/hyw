# Project: Nature Adventure AR App

## Overview
An interactive mobile game for children that combines real-world exploration with a mysterious narrative. Users follow a linear sequence of stops guided by GPS and environmental clues.

## Core Mechanics
- **GPS Check:** The app checks location only when opened at a potential stop (no background geofencing).
- **AR Storytelling:** Upon arrival, the camera view detects stability and triggers an AR character to advance the story.
- **Environmental Clues:** Clues point to real landmarks or use in-app tools like a magical compass.
- **Progression:** A fixed sequence of stops leading to a grand finale.

## Visual Direction
- **Style:** Epic, Mysterious, Adventurous.
- **Theme:** Nature, Magic, Exploration.
- **Target Audience:** Children and families on outdoor trips.

## Key Screens to Design
1. **Intro & Journey Map:** Setting the scene and showing progress.
2. **Location Verification:** Checking if the player has arrived at the correct spot.
3. **AR Character Encounter:** The immersive story moment via camera view.
4. **The Next Clue:** A mysterious hint or interactive compass for the next leg of the journey.
